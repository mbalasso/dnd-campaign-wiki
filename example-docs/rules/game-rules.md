# Game rules

The house rules **currently in effect**. Older versions are gone for good.

## Potions
Drinking any potion is a bonus action. If you use your action instead, a healing potion heals its maximum.

## One spell slot per turn
You can spend at most one spell slot per turn, reaction included. Spells cast without a slot (racial traits, items, scrolls) don't count toward this limit.

## Critical hits
On a critical hit, roll the extra damage dice normally and add the maximum of one die.

!!! info "Changing a rule"
    Rule changes are proposed at the end of a session and voted on by the whole table.
