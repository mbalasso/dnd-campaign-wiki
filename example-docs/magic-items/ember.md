# Magic items of the Ember Company

### **Lantern of the Drowned**

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

While lit, this green-glass lantern sheds dim light in a 20-foot radius. Undead within the light can't hide, and you know the direction of the nearest undead within 1 mile. The lantern burns for 1 hour per day.

### **Tess's Weighted Purse**

*Wondrous item, common*

This purse always feels slightly heavier than it should. Once per day, when you search it, you find 1d4 copper pieces that weren't there before. Nobody knows whose they are.
