# Session 0

Our heroes meet on the deck of a leaking fishing boat bound for Greywater. **Mira**, human fighter and former city guard. **Dorn**, dwarf cleric of the forge. **Tess**, halfling rogue with a suspiciously heavy purse. The captain swears the fog is ordinary. It is not.

On the beach, drowned sailors rise from the surf. The party survives, barely, and climbs to the village on the cliffs, where **Elder Hanne** offers shelter in exchange for answers: why have the dead stopped resting?
