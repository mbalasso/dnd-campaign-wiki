# The Ambush at Greywater Pass

A hundred miles inland, a second group is hired to escort a salt caravan to the coast. **Kael**, elf wizard who reads too much. **Rook**, half-orc barbarian who reads too little. **Isa**, tiefling bard who reads people.

At Greywater Pass the caravan is ambushed by bandits wearing the gull sigil of the **Saltbrokers' Guild**, the very guild that hired them. Rook wins the fight. Isa wins the interrogation. Kael finds the map in the bandit leader's boot: an X on the cliffs above Greywater village.
