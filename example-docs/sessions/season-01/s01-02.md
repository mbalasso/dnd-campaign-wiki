# Two Fires on the Cliff

The two parties meet for the first time, swords drawn, on the cliff marked on Kael's map. The Ember Company thinks the strangers raised the dead. The Tidebound think the strangers work for the Guild. Tess and Isa, both reaching for the same purse, are the first to start laughing.

Comparing notes around two campfires, they piece it together: since [[s01-01|the ambush at the pass]], the Guild has been shipping something up the coast inside the salt barrels, and wherever the barrels go, the dead follow.
