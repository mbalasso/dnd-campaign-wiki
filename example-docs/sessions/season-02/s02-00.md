# The Saltbrokers' Ledger

Months later, the Tidebound infiltrate the Guild's counting house in the capital. Isa talks her way past the clerks, Kael copies the ledger page by page, and Rook stands very still in a corridor pretending to be a statue, which works far better than anyone expected.

The ledger names a buyer for the barrels: someone signing only as **the Tidekeeper**.
